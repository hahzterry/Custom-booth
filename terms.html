What the photobooth is

LUMEE BOOTH is a browser-based live event experience. A host creates an event entrance and Look, then guests take three photographs once. All three make the Photo Strip and Moving Polaroid; the guest picks their favourite for the Magazine Cover. Installation as a web app is optional; an account is not required.

Using it

You may use the photobooth for your own events. Please do not use it to photograph people who have not agreed to be photographed, and please respect the law and the venue you are in. You are responsible for the photos you take and for what you do with them afterwards.

Your photos are yours

You keep all rights in the photographs you take. We do not receive them, do not store them and make no claim to them. See Privacy for exactly where they live.

Your data is local to the devices you use

This is the most important practical thing to understand. The booth stores your photos and event settings in your browser on the device you are using. That means:

  • There is no backup. We cannot recover anything for you.
  • Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or your browser reclaiming space can remove them permanently.
  • Your booth does not automatically follow you to another device or browser, and there is no durable Personal-event record or public event link on a MyBishBash server.

A Setup Pass can deliberately copy event configuration to another device. It carries no photographs, logo, paid entitlement or running event clock, and importing it leaves the event as a draft. It is host setup information, not a cloud backup or guest-facing event link. If the photos matter, save or share them off the device while you still can.

Guest PIN

An optional four-digit Guest PIN is a lightweight local gate for the event entrance. It is checked on the event device, not by a server, and is not secure password protection. Do not reuse an important PIN or rely on it to protect sensitive information. Repeated failed attempts are briefly delayed, but clearing local browser state can also clear that local protection.

Availability

The photobooth is provided as it is, without a promise that it will always be available, uninterrupted or free of faults. It depends on your device and browser supporting secure camera access, and on your device having room to store what it creates. Moving Polaroid additionally depends on a usable on-device video encoder. Safari and iPadOS support varies by version and device; where a moving file cannot be created, the booth keeps the finished still available rather than claiming motion exists. Native Share and download behaviour also belongs to the device and browser. We may change or withdraw features.
